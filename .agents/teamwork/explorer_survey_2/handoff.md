2. **Step 2 (Direct Dealer Flow Alignment):** Replacing "Dealer confirmation is required before reservation" and "quoted separately" with "Direct Texacars dealer inventory with transparent out-the-door pricing" aligns the static pages with the direct Texacars dealer purchasing workflow (Context item 1 of `ORIGINAL_REQUEST.md`).
3. **Step 3 (Safety of Regeneration):** Unit tests in `tests/test_public_pages.py` verify catalog structure, schema tags, and absence of private supplier data, but do not assert the old broker strings (Observation 3). Therefore, updating the template strings in `tools/build.py` will not break existing tests.

Replacing the disclaimer leaves `Vehicle price` and all SEO schema intact, ensuring full compatibility with Playwright smoke suites.
