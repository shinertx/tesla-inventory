# Online-first model and competitive comparison

The $250 refundable reservation remains part of the buying flow.

Fully online vehicle purchases are possible. Texas permits a dealer to sell online without the buyer personally visiting the dealership.

The actual selling dealer, transaction structure, customer documents, and destination requirements still determine the supported workflow.

[Texas Occupations Code, Chapter 2301](https://tcss.legis.texas.gov/Docs/OC/htm/OC.2301.htm)

Vroom announced the wind-down of its used-vehicle ecommerce operations on January 22, 2024.

[Vroom announcement](https://ir.vroom.com/news-releases/news-release-details/vroom-announces-wind-down-ecommerce-used-vehicle-operations/)

This marketplace avoids purchasing cars into platform inventory. That can reduce inventory financing and depreciation exposure compared with an owned-inventory model.

It places greater importance on dealer commitments, inspection consistency, fulfillment, and collection of the agreed platform spread.

Carvana's owned logistics and reconditioning network supports direct operating control. A dealer network must establish comparable customer reliability through contracts, integrations, and execution.

[Carvana 2025 annual report](https://investors.carvana.com/~/media/Files/C/Carvana-IR/documents/cvna-2025-annual-report.pdf)

The model is promising to test. It is not proven superior because it owns no vehicles.

Evaluate collected contribution per Tesla, demand cost, dealer acceptance, quote accuracy, delivery performance, returns, and fee collection.

Retail pricing should reflect supported vehicle value and buyer response. Source pricing should reflect the lowest agreed dealer proceeds that preserve reliable fulfillment.

There is no reason for a uniform $1,500 spread limit. There is also no evidence yet for the maximum achievable spread or the optimal customer price.
