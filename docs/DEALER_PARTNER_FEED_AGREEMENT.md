## Why Non-Tesla Franchise & Independent Dealers Opt Into Our Direct Feed

Non-Tesla franchise stores (Ford, Chevrolet, Toyota, Honda, BMW, Mercedes-Benz) take used Teslas on trade-in that sit `45–60+` days before going to wholesale auction with `$600–$1,200` in auction/transport fees.

**Volts & VINs** moves your aged or fresh clean-title Tesla trade-ins **at `$0` cost to your store**:
1. **Zero Listing Fees, Zero Retail Headaches:** We never charge subscription or lead fees. When our online EV buyer locks your unit, **our licensed Texas GDN buys the unit directly from your store via B2B wholesale wire** (`Texas Resale Certificate Form 01-339`).
2. **Guaranteed Clean B2B Exit:** No retail buyer walk-ins, no test-drive tire kickers, no post-sale Tesla app/FSD customer support calls—your store executes a single wholesale bill of sale + `VTR-136` title reassignment to our GDN, and our insured carrier picks up at your service drive.
3. **Pre-Agreed Wholesale Discount (`$500–$1,000` Back of Retail Ask):** Because your store saves reconditioning carry, floorplan interest, auction seller fees, and retail sales commissions, Partner Dealers grant a standard wholesale net schedule (`$750` below advertised internet retail ask, or custom net price in `vAuto` / `HomeNet`) with zero retail doc fees.

1. **Automated DMS / Inventory Feed Export:** Supplying Dealership authorizes its inventory syndicator (**Cox Automotive `HomeNet` / `vAuto`**, ** Reynolds**, **CDK**, or **DealerCenter**) to syndicate active clean-title Tesla VINs, odometer readings, equipment descriptions, wholesale/internet pricing, and **un-watermarked vehicle photography** to the Volts & VINs Partner Network feed.
2. **24-Hour Wholesale Courtesy Hold:** Upon receipt of a written Wholesale Purchase Intent from Fulfilling GDN referencing an active retail buyer lock, Supplying Dealership agrees to confirm physical availability, clean title / `ELT` lien payoff status, and a 24-hour wholesale wire hold during normal business hours.
3. **Title & Lien Payoff SLA:** Upon receipt of Fulfilling GDN's cleared wholesale wire, Supplying Dealership agrees to release the vehicle to Fulfilling GDN's insured carrier (`BOL`) and overnight-courier the reassigned clean title (or initiate `ELT` payoff within 24 hours, delivering clear title within `10` business days).
