## 1. Regulatory Structure (43 TAC § 215.85, § 215.244 & § 215.245 Compliance)

1. **Dealership Is the Sole Seller & DBA Registrant:**
   - Dealership is the sole licensed motor-vehicle seller of record under Texas Occupations Code Chapter 2301 and 43 TAC Chapter 215.
   - Prior to public brand launch under Dealership's license, Dealership shall file an Assumed Name Certificate (**DBA: `"Volts & VINs"`**) with the Texas Secretary of State (`$25` fee) and add `"Volts & VINs"` to Dealership's **TxDMV `eLICENSING`** record within 30 days.
   - All retail Buyer's Orders, Retail Installment Sale Contracts (`RISC`), wholesale title reassignments (**TxDMV Form VTR-136**), `TxDMV webDEALER` buyer e-tags, and `6.25%` motor-vehicle tax remittances are executed strictly in Dealership's licensed name and GDN.
2. **Bona Fide W-2 Employee Exemption (`43 TAC § 215.85(a)`):**
   - Texas does not separately license individual motor-vehicle salespersons; Dealership employs **Ben McMillan** directly on Dealership payroll (`Form W-4` / `Form I-9` / `Form W-2`) as **Director of Online EV Acquisitions & Sales**.
   - As a bona fide W-2 employee acting exclusively under Dealership's GDN authority and supervision, Director is authorized to negotiate dealer-to-dealer wholesale Tesla acquisitions, issue 1-on-1 written buyer quotes (`43 TAC § 215.244(1)(B)`), coordinate F&I/warranty menus, and earn W-2 sales commissions on completed Dealership sales.
3. **Strict Separation of Software Vendor (`43 TAC § 215.85(b)`):**
   - **JENNI PRO INC** licenses catalog hosting, OCR photo screening, and Deal Desk software for a **fixed flat monthly software fee (`$500/month`, waivable during pilot)** that never varies by leads, reservations, vehicle prices, or closed unit volume.
   - **JENNI PRO INC never sets vehicle prices, never holds buyer deposits, and never receives per-car fees or profit splits.**
4. **Two-Step Partner Network Listing & Deposit Rule (`43 TAC § 215.245(e)` & `§ 215.244(1)(B)`):**

## 2. Deal Clearing, Floorplan/Escrow Flow & W-2 Commission Schedule

### A. Wholesale Acquisition & Retail Settlement
1. **1-on-1 Quote & Wholesale Lock:** Upon a `$0` price-lock request on `voltsandvins.com`, Director contacts the supplying franchise/independent dealer under Dealership's GDN + **Texas Resale Certificate (`Form 01-339`)** to lock wholesale net pricing, verify clean title/lien payoff (`ELT`), and issue Dealership's 1-on-1 Out-the-Door Buyer's Order.
2. **Funding Path (Zero Unlicensed Float):**
   - **Cash / Credit Union Wire:** Buyer or buyer's credit union wires full Out-the-Door funds into **Dealership's bank account** (or licensed title/escrow clearing account); Dealership wires wholesale net to supplying dealer, receives overnight reassigned title (`VTR-136`), issues `webDEALER` tag, and dispatches insured carrier transport.
   - **Dealer-Arranged Financing (`Dealertrack` / `RouteOne` / `CUDL`):** Dealership acquires wholesale unit via Dealership's floorplan line (`NextGear` / `AFC`) upon signed `RISC` + verified down payment, submits reassigned title copy to lender, and pays off floorplan immediately upon lender wire receipt.

### B. Dealership House Pack & Director W-2 Commission
For each closed, delivered, and funded vehicle sale originated by Director's division:
- **Dealership Retains ("House Pack"):**
  1. `100%` of state motor-vehicle taxes and official `TxDMV` title/registration fees (held in trust for remittance), plus
  2. Actual third-party carrier transport, floorplan daily interest (if any), and Stripe processing fees, plus
  3. **`$500.00` per-unit Dealership House Pack** (reducing to `$350.00/unit` above `25` units/month).
- **Director W-2 Commission (`90%` Net Front + Back Gross Above House Pack):**
  - Dealership pays Director as **W-2 wages/commission** (subject to standard payroll withholding) **`100%` of remaining Net Deal Gross** above the House Pack (`Vehicle Spread + Documentary Fee + Lender F&I Reserve + EV Extended Service Contract / GAP Net Margin`) on Dealership's bi-weekly or weekly commission payroll run.
